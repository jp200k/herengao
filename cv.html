CV

Selected chronology of major events.

Inquiries → Works →
  1. 2021

    Held a solo oil painting exhibition at the Rongbaozhai Academy.

  2. 2018

    Invited to attend the establishment ceremony of the Cambodia Vattanac Bank (by US Sun Company) and served as a guest award presenter at the calligraphy and painting symposium.

    Commenced studies in the He Rengao National Exhibition Creation Class, part of the Senior Research Program for Famous Calligraphers and Painters at the Academy of Arts & Design, Tsinghua University.

  3. 2017

    Participated in the "Top 30 Chinese Calligraphy and Painting Masters" national touring exhibition.

    Oil painting works were acquired for the Rockefeller family collection.

    Organized the "Converging Classics · Seeking the Way in Dunhuang" Famous Artists Invitation Exhibition.

  4. 2016

    Invited to New York City, USA, to hold a solo touring exhibition.

    Signed a professional contract with the US Sun Company.

    Invited to Russia to participate in a joint exhibition.

  5. 2015

    Solo exhibition held in New York, organized by the US-China Cultural and Art Exchange Association.

    Held the solo exhibition "Seeking the Way of Heaven and Earth: Legacy of the Sui and Tang Dynasties" in Changsha, Hunan.

  6. 2014–2015

    Studied in the 2nd Master Class under Fan Yang at the Fenghuangling Academy.

  7. 2013

    Enrolled in the Jia Youfu Advanced Seminar at the Central Academy of Fine Arts (CAFA).

  8. 2011–2012

    Studied in the Senior Research Program at the Academy of Arts & Design, Tsinghua University.

    Officially joined the China Artists Association.

  9. 2009

    Painted ceramics in Liling, Hunan, and pioneered the "Heavy Glaze Ceramic Painting" technique.

  10. 2006

    Held the landscape painting lightbox exhibition titled "River Breeze and Colorful Clouds" at Huangxing South Road Commercial Street, Changsha.

  11. 2004

    The work The Majestic Taihang Mountains won the Excellence Award in the "National Calligraphy and Painting Competition Commemorating the 100th Anniversary of Comrade Deng Xiaoping's Birth" (hosted by People's Daily) and was included in the official collection.

  12. 2001

    The work Mountains and Rivers of China was exhibited at the National Art Museum of China (NAMOC) and selected for the Collection of Fine Works by Deans of National Chinese Painting Academies (hosted by the China Federation of Literary and Art Circles).

  13. 2000

    The work Wild Mountain Wind was selected for the Collection of Fine Works by Famous Chinese Calligraphers and Painters of the 20th Century.

  14. 1999

    Held first solo exhibition, "Ink Bamboo, Figures, and Calligraphy," at the Shenzhen Artist Gallery.

  15. 1998

    Appointed Vice President of the Shenzhen Nanguo Academy of Painting.

  16. 1966

    Participated in creating portraits of Chairman Mao and transcribing Mao's quotations for public art initiatives.
